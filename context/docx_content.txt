College Newsletter Platform
Product Requirements Document (PRD)

Version 1.0 | 26 Jan 2026

1. Overview
A calm, premium, low-noise newsletter platform for college students. Instead of long reads, every update is condensed into a 2-3 minute summary and organized by department, industry insights, and student success stories.
2. Problem Statement
Students struggle to read long content due to poor attention and time constraints.
The real competition is high-attention platforms (Instagram/YouTube), not other newsletter apps.
If relevancy is slightly off or language feels difficult, students drop immediately.
Students want visible growth (progress) to stay consistent.
3. Goals & Non-Goals
Goals
Deliver department-relevant learning in 2-3 minutes per item.
Make the homepage immediately useful (no hero section) with 'must-read' news.
Auto-process ~1000+ sources into weekly buckets with consistent quality.
Keep the experience calm and premium: limited choices, minimal noise, clear hierarchy.
Non-Goals
Competing on infinite scrolling or rage/engagement-based content loops.
Publishing unverified charts/claims without traceable sources.

5. Brand & Experience Principles
Calm in a noisy world: minimal colors, controlled density, no clutter.
Nostalgic + premium: editorial feel, consistent typography, predictable layouts.
High signal only: fewer items, better curation.
Readable by default: bullets over paragraphs, simple language.

8. Content Processing Pipeline (Automation)
Scrape & ingest ~1000 sources into a standard raw format (title, url, date, raw text).
Clean & extract article body (remove nav/footer/subscribe blocks).
Deduplicate (exact + near-duplicate) to avoid repetitive content.
Classify: department, category (trend/job/story), difficulty, priority score.
Summarize to 2-3 minute format: hook line, why it matters, 3-6 bullets, action step.
Generate visuals: charts/diagrams via template renderer; AI imagery only for breaking/breakthrough.
Assign weekly bucket (YYYY-W##) and auto-publish into Home / Weekly / Dept pages.
9. Read Format Standard (2-3 Minutes)
Headline (max 8 words)
Why it matters (1 line)
3-6 bullets (one idea per bullet, short lines)
Optional: quick example (1-2 lines)
Action step (1 line)
10. Visualizer (Charts + Explainers)
To stay premium and accurate at scale, charts are rendered via standard templates. AI-generated images are used only for Breaking News and Great Breakthroughs.
Chart templates: Bar / Line / Donut (Apple/SaaS style: minimal grid, clear labels, rounded container).
Concept visuals when data is missing: impact map, 1-2-3 steps, timeline.
AI imagery: minimal abstract backgrounds with reserved space for chart overlay.
Per article: max 1-2 visuals to protect reading time.
11. Recommendations & Right-Side Suggestions
Reading page includes a calm, limited sidebar with a tab-style selector (YouTube-like).
Top suggestion chips (4): Suggested for you | Mech content | Related: <Topic Name> | Same: <Topic Name>
Maximum 7 suggestions shown at once.
Candidate sources: same department, similar topic, weekly best, relevant stories.
Ranking emphasizes: similarity, department match, freshness (this week), quality, user preferences.
12. Gamification (Learning-First, Minimal)
Weekly goal meter (ex: 0/7 summaries).
Completion nudge: 'You're one step away from completing' on exit intent.
Streaks + gentle badges (no loud popups).
Skill tags accumulate quietly (for future skill tree).
13. Admin & Operations (MVP)
Source management: add/remove sources, monitor failures.
Review queue: low-confidence summaries require approval.
Pin/feature: select breaking stories for homepage spotlight.
Weekly digest auto-build with manual override.
14. Success Metrics
Weekly active users (WAU) and retention (D7/W4).
Average session length (target: 2-6 minutes).
Completion rate per read (target: >60%).
Save/share rate (signal of usefulness).
Relevancy feedback rate (downvotes/reports).
15. Risks & Mitigations
Risk: incorrect summaries or misleading charts -> Mitigation: confidence score + fall back to concept visuals.
Risk: content overload -> Mitigation: cap cards shown, weekly curation, prioritize high-signal.
Risk: inconsistent visuals -> Mitigation: template-first visuals, AI only for highlights.
Risk: low relevancy -> Mitigation: strict department tagging and topic similarity thresholds.
16. MVP Scope (First Release)
News-first homepage + category filters
Industrial Insights page
Departments hub + department detail page
Student Stories page
2-3 minute summary cards
Template charts + 1-3 highlighted AI visuals per week
Right-side suggestions with 4 chips
