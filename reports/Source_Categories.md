# Content Source Categories for College Newsletter Platform

**Version:** 1.0  
**Date:** January 31, 2026  
**Purpose:** Comprehensive categorization of information sources for holistic student development


# Content Philosophy

The newsletter aims to bridge the gap between academic education and real-world readiness. Sources are selected based on:

- Practical applicability (can students use this today?)
- Career acceleration (will this help them get hired?)
- Life preparedness (things colleges don't teach)
- Global awareness (understanding the world they live in)
- Opportunity creation (events, competitions, funding)

# Content Distribution Strategy

## By Student Year

**First Year (20-30% weightage):**
- Life skills (financial, productivity)
- General tech awareness
- Event opportunities (hackathons, competitions)
- Soft skills development

**Second Year (40-50% weightage):**
- Technical deep dives
- Internship opportunities
- Open source programs
- Project building resources

**Third Year (60-70% weightage):**
- Job market intelligence
- Interview preparation
- Company-specific insights
- Placement opportunities

**Fourth Year/Final (50-60% weightage):**
- Full-time job openings
- Higher studies opportunities
- Entrepreneurial paths
- Long-term career planning


## By Department (Customizable)

**Computer Science:**
- Higher weight on AI/ML research, open source, system design

**Electronics:**
- Higher weight on hardware news, IoT, semiconductor updates

**Mechanical:**
- Higher weight on manufacturing tech, CAD, aerospace

**Civil:**
- Higher weight on construction tech, smart cities, sustainability

**Business/Management:**
- Higher weight on startup ecosystem, VC news, business strategy


## By Interest Area (Student-Selected)

**Aspiring Entrepreneurs:**
- Startup ecosystem, funding news, indie hacker stories, pitch competitions

**Research-Oriented:**
- Research papers, PhD positions, conference alerts, lab openings

**Job-Focused:**
- Interview prep, company updates, skill demand, job openings

**Generalists:**
- Balanced mix of all categories, emphasis on life skills and global news


# Source Selection Criteria

Every source must meet at least 3 of these criteria:

1. **Actionable:** Can a student act on this information today?
2. **Career-Relevant:** Does this help with employment or entrepreneurship?
3. **Educational:** Does this teach something colleges don't?
4. **Timely:** Is this information current and relevant?
5. **Credible:** Is the source trustworthy and authoritative?
6. **Accessible:** Is the content understandable without deep expertise?
7. **Diverse:** Does this represent different perspectives?
8. **Practical:** Can this be applied in real projects or decisions?


# Content Freshness Tiers

**Breaking (Immediate notification):**
- Major tech company outages
- Critical security vulnerabilities
- Major funding rounds
- Urgent deadlines (last 24 hours)

**Daily (In newsletter):**
- Tech news
- Job postings

**Weekly (Round-up):**

- Tutorial collections

**Monthly (Feature):**
