Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

NEWS DAY ("we", "us", "our") is a department-specific news aggregation platform built for college students. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information when you use our platform:

  • Account information: email address, full name, department, year of study, college name, and graduation year provided during registration.
  • Reading history: articles you read, reading duration, and completion percentage to personalize your feed.
  • Saved articles: content you bookmark or save to collections.
  • Feedback: likes, dislikes, and reports you submit on articles.
  • Preferences: interests, content preferences, notification settings, and weekly reading goals.
  • Browser data: we use localStorage to cache content, store your session token, and remember your department selection and UI preferences.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Personalized news feed: we use your department, interests, and reading history to curate relevant content for you.
  • Department-specific content: your department selection ensures you see news relevant to your field of study.
  • Usage analytics: we track reading patterns, streaks, and skill badges to help you measure your learning progress.
  • AI-generated summaries: article content is processed through AI to generate concise summaries for your convenience.
  • Platform improvement: aggregated, anonymized usage data helps us improve content curation and user experience.

3. Data Storage & Security

Your data is hosted on cloud servers and encrypted at rest. We use industry-standard security practices including:

  • Passwords are hashed using bcrypt before storage — we never store plaintext passwords.
  • Authentication tokens (JWT) are used for session management with automatic expiry.
  • All API communication uses HTTPS encryption in transit.
  • Rate limiting is enforced on authentication endpoints to prevent brute-force attacks.

4. Third-Party Services

We integrate with the following third-party services:

  • Unsplash — provides article feature images. Unsplash may collect usage analytics when images are loaded. See Unsplash Privacy Policy.
  • Pollinations AI — generates article summaries. Article titles and content are sent to their API for processing. See Pollinations AI.
  • Google Fonts — loads DM Sans and Inter typefaces. Google may collect usage data when fonts are served. See Google Privacy Policy.

5. Cookies & Local Storage

We use the following browser storage mechanisms:

  • Session authentication: JWT tokens are stored in localStorage to keep you logged in across page visits.
  • Preferences: your selected department, theme settings, and cookie consent status are stored in localStorage.
  • Cached content: article data and AI summaries are cached locally to improve loading speed and enable limited offline access.

We do not use third-party tracking cookies. You can clear localStorage at any time through your browser settings, though this will log you out.

6. Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access your data: view your profile, reading history, and saved articles through your account dashboard.
  • Delete your account: you can permanently delete your account and all associated data (reading history, saves, feedback) at any time. Deletion is immediate and irreversible.
  • Export your data: contact us to request a full export of your personal data in a machine-readable format.
  • Rectification: update your profile information at any time through your account settings.

7. Data Retention

  • Account data is retained for as long as your account is active.
  • Upon account deletion, all personal data (profile, reading history, saves, and feedback) is permanently deleted within 30 days.
  • Anonymized, aggregated analytics data may be retained indefinitely as it cannot be linked back to individual users.

8. Children's Privacy

NEWS DAY is designed for college students. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 16. If you believe a child under 16 has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make significant changes, we will notify registered users via email. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page indicates when the policy was last revised.
